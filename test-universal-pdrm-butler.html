Neural Crest Formation

Neural crest cells are a remarkable population of cells that arise during embryonic development in vertebrates. These cells originate from the ectoderm at the border between the neural plate and the non-neural ectoderm.

The formation of neural crest cells begins during neurulation, when the neural plate folds to form the neural tube. At the dorsal aspect of the neural tube, a population of cells delaminates and begins to migrate throughout the embryo.

This process is regulated by a network of transcription factors including Snail, Slug, FoxD3, and Sox10...
